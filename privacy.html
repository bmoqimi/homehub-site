HomeHub privacy policy

HomeHub is a private app that runs on the owner's own computer for one household. It has no other users.

When the owner connects Google Photos, HomeHub uploads photos the household chooses to an album it creates, and reads photos the owner picks. The data stays on the owner's computer and in the owner's Google account. It is not shared, sold or sent to anyone else.

The owner can disconnect at any time in HomeHub's settings or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
